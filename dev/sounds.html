Dead End: sound audition

Every recorded sound and music track in the game, with the event it plays for and its credit. In game plays the sound the way the game does (level, random pitch, layers). Synth plays the synthesized version the game falls back to. The numbered buttons play each file on its own.

To swap a sound, replace its file in audio/sfx/ or audio/music/, then update SAMPLES or TRACKS in src/audio.js and audio/CREDITS.md.

Audio starts after you press the button.
